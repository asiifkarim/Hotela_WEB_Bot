# Glossary of Hotela terms

## How to use this glossary

The knowledge base uses one term for each concept. When a visitor uses a different word, answer with the word they used and the term Hotela uses, so the same question never gets two different names.

## Business and location terms

| Term Hotela uses | Words visitors use | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Branch | Property, outlet, location, site, unit, hotel, restaurant | One operating location on an account. Hotela prices plans per branch, not per room or per user. |
| Multi-branch management | Multi-property, chain, group | Running several branches from one login, with a Branch filter on most screens. |
| Plan | Package, subscription, tier, license | One of Hotela Dine, Hotela Stay or Hotela Suite. |

## People terms

| Term Hotela uses | Words visitors use | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Customer | Guest, client, diner, patron | A person recorded in the customer list; the same record is referenced by reservations, invoices and events. |
| Staff | Employee, team member, user | A person given access to the dashboard, invited with role-based access. |
| Owner | Admin, manager, proprietor | The account holder who can see the financial overview and manage plans. |
| Front desk | Reception | The screen where check-ins, check-outs and guest requests are handled. |

## Booking terms

| Term Hotela uses | Words visitors use | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Reservation | Booking, room booking | A room booking with a check-in date, a check-out date and a status. |
| Table reservation | Table booking | A restaurant table booked for a diner; a Hotela Dine feature. |
| Event | Function, wedding, banquet | A booking tracked in Event Management with guest count, revenue and pending balance. |
| Check-in / check-out | Arrival / departure | The status changes a reservation goes through on the arrival and departure dates. |
| Channel manager | OTA sync, rate sync, booking channel integration | The feature that syncs availability and rates across OTAs and booking platforms. |
| OTA | Booking site, travel site, online travel agency | An external booking platform such as a travel website. Which ones Hotela connects to is not published. |

## Money terms

| Term Hotela uses | Words visitors use | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Invoice | Bill, receipt, voucher | Billing record; Hotel Invoices for stays, Restaurant Invoices and Food Invoices for dining. |
| Transaction | Payment, entry, ledger line | A money record with ID, description, amount and status. |
| Expenses | Costs, spending | Money spent, recorded with name, category, vendor, source and date. |
| Petty cash | Cash float, small cash | Small day-to-day cash, shown with Total Petty Cash, Total Expenses and Available Amount. |
| Outstanding balance | Due, unpaid amount | Money not yet received; on events it appears as the pending balance. |
| Service charge | Service fee | A charge added to a restaurant invoice next to the discount. |
| POS | Till, cash register, point of sale | The restaurant billing side of Hotela Dine. |
| Revenue | Sales, income, turnover | Money received, reported per branch and per period. |
