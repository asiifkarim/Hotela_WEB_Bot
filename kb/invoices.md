# Invoices

## The invoice screens Hotela has

Hotela keeps hotel and restaurant billing apart:

- Hotel Invoices, under Accounts, for room stays and other hotel charges.
- Restaurant Invoices, under Accounts, for restaurant bills.
- Food Invoices, under the Restaurant menu, for the day-to-day restaurant sales list.

## Hotel invoices

- Hotel Invoices cover room stays and other hotel charges booked on the account.
- Payments recorded against a reservation appear in Hotela Transactions as Reservation Payment.
- Staff can filter the invoice list by date range, customer name and branch.
- The financial totals behind invoices, such as revenue and outstanding balance, are shown on the Transactions screen; see the Finances file.

## Restaurant and food invoices

- Food Invoices list customer name, total items, total discount and total amount.
- Restaurant Invoices record each item, then subtotal, discount, service charge, grand total, received amount and return amount.
- The difference between the grand total and the received amount is what the customer has not yet paid.
- Restaurant invoices can be filtered by date range, customer name and branch.
- The return amount records money handed back to the customer.

## What is not documented about invoices

A customer-facing invoice page, a PDF download and email delivery of invoices are not documented, so the assistant must not promise any of them.

## Not confirmed for invoices

- Tax or government invoicing: tax configuration, GST or VAT fields, and e-invoicing integration are not documented.
- Exporting invoices or reports to PDF or Excel is not documented.
- Accounting software integration and invoice synchronisation are not documented.
- Payment gateway or online payment collection against an invoice is not documented.
