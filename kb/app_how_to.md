# Hotela App: How To

## Getting started with Hotela

The website describes four steps:

1. Visit the Hotela website and see how Hotela fits the business.
2. Subscribe to a plan and enter the property details to reach the dashboard.
3. Configure the property: add rooms, tables and menus.
4. Give the team access by inviting managers and staff with role-based access.

The website states that most properties are live within a day.

## Signing up

- A visitor can start from the signup page at https://portal.hotelaapp.com/signup.
- At signup a business creates an account, chooses a plan and enters the property details to reach the dashboard.
- Trial terms, free onboarding and the six-month billing period are in the Policies file.

## Branches and multi-property access

- Hotela Suite includes Multi-branch Management.
- A business can manage multiple properties from one account: hotels, restaurants and cities sit behind one login.
- Users switch between hotels, restaurants and cities from one dashboard, with role-based access for each team member.
- Branches are created in Settings under Branches, with a name and a location.
- Most screens carry a Branch filter: reservations, rooms, room categories, transactions, invoices, expenses, food invoices, events, staff, inventory and customers.
- When an owner needs more branches than the plan allows, the pricing page asks them to contact Hotela after signup. The exact branch limit per plan is not published.

## Dashboard walkthrough

- The home screen gives an owner a summary of finances, bookings, complaints and upcoming check-ins.
- It shows Total Profit, Total Expenses and Available Amount, plus the number of bookings, available rooms today, total rooms and upcoming check-ins.
- Complaints are shown as total, resolved and unresolved.
- A Statistics chart shows reservations and revenue for a chosen year, and a Transaction Report can be produced for a chosen date range.

## Staff and payroll

- The website describes HR Management as staff schedules, payroll and records for every department in one place.
- Hotela shows Total Employees, Active Staff, On Leave and New Joiners.
- The Employees tab lists staff with name, employee ID, role, branch, joining date and status, and can be filtered by search, role, status and branch.
- Owners add a new employee with the Add New Employee button.
- The other tabs are Current Payroll, Salary Advances, Payroll History and Final Settlement.
- Salaries and advances also appear in Accounts under Expenses; see the Finances file.
- Managers and staff are invited with role-based access.

## Inventory

- Hotela shows Total Items, Low Stock Items and Total Categories.
- Items can be searched by name and filtered by category.
- Each item shows category, quantity, unit and status.
- Owners can add items and increase or decrease quantities.

## Which features are confirmed, and which are not

**Confirmed by the Hotela dashboard screens**

| Area | Confirmed |
|---|---|
| Reservations | List, filters, statuses, Manage action, Available Rooms tab |
| Rooms | Room list, bed types, room categories with cost and occupancy |
| Housekeeping | Dirty rooms list, Available Rooms tab |
| Restaurant | Food Invoices, Restaurant Invoices with discount and service charge |
| Events | Event list, guest count, revenue, pending balance, event expenses, calendar |
| Accounts | Transactions, Hotel and Restaurant Invoices, Expenses, Petty Cash |
| Staff | Employees, Current Payroll, Salary Advances, Payroll History, Final Settlement |
| Inventory | Items, categories, quantity, low stock count |
| Customers | Customer list with name, CNIC, email, contact |
| Branches | Multi-branch management, Branch filter on most screens |
| Dashboard | Financial overview, booking overview, complaints, upcoming check-ins, statistics, transaction report |
| Analytics | Occupancy, revenue and performance insights |
| Subscription | Plans, upgrade, renew, subscription history |

**Confirmed by the pricing page and `plans.json`**

Plan features are governed by `plans.json`; the plan table is in the Policies file. The channel manager is confirmed for Hotela Stay and Hotela Suite (see the Bookings file for what it syncs and what is not published).

**Stated on the website, not documented in the product screens**

The website describes these, so they may be repeated as website statements, but the details must not be expanded:

- Offline-ready POS and front desk
- A Windows and Mac desktop app
- Real-time cloud sync between devices
- Works on every device, including access from a phone

**Not confirmed: do not claim these**

- A mobile app for iOS or Android. The website footer shows Google Play and App Store buttons, but they are placeholders that do not link to a store listing.
- Annual plan prices. The pricing page offers annual billing but publishes only six-month prices.
- Discounts, refund terms and detailed cancellation steps.
- The exact branch limit for each plan, and any price for additional branches.
- Tax or government invoicing integration, and accounting software integration.
- Report or invoice export to PDF or Excel.
- A payment gateway or online payment collection, and SMS, WhatsApp or email notifications to guests.
- QR menus, dedicated POS or kitchen display hardware, and API access for developers.
- An Urdu language interface, and data import from other systems.
- Attendance, leave tracking, payslips, purchase orders, supplier management, customer import and customer segmentation.
