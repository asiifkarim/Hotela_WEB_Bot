# Policies

## The three Hotela plans

| Plan | Positioned for | Features |
|---|---|---|
| Hotela Dine | Restaurants | Table reservations, POS & billing, Menu & kitchen tickets, Online orders, Restaurant analytics |
| Hotela Stay | Hotels | Room reservations, Front desk & check-in, Housekeeping, Channel manager, Hotel analytics |
| Hotela Suite | Hotel and restaurant together | Everything in Stay & Dine, Unified dashboard, Multi-branch & roles, Channel manager, Priority 24/7 support |

- Hotela Suite is the full platform: it includes the Hotela Stay features and the Hotela Dine features, and adds a unified dashboard, multi-branch management with roles, the channel manager and priority 24/7 support. A business does not buy Stay and Dine separately to run a hotel and a restaurant on Suite.
- `plans.json` is the only source for plan names, plan features and prices, and it mirrors the pricing page. A plan feature that is not listed there must not be claimed; say the details are not confirmed and offer the Hotela team.
- Which plan fits: a restaurant that needs table reservations, POS billing, menus and kitchen tickets looks at Hotela Dine; a hotel that needs rooms, front desk and housekeeping looks at Hotela Stay; a business that needs both, or runs several branches, looks at Hotela Suite.
- Prices, the billing period and the branch selector are in the Rooms and Pricing file.

## The Hotela subscription screen

- Under Settings, the Subscription & Billing screen shows the available plans and marks the current plan.
- The current plan has a View / Renew button, and the other plans have Upgrade buttons.
- A subscription history table lists each renewal or reactivation with date, plan, duration, amount, payment method, status and period.

## Trial, fees and cancellation

- Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial. The pricing page and the home page both state this.
- The home page states that the trial gives full access to every feature, with no charge to begin.
- Every plan includes free onboarding, and there are no setup fees.
- Customers can cancel anytime with no commitment.
- What happens on day 8 of the trial, and whether a payment method is needed to start, are not published, so they are not confirmed.
- Refund terms and the detailed cancellation steps are not published, so they are not confirmed.

## Security and reliability

- The website states 99.9% uptime, data encrypted in transit and at rest, automated backups and granular permissions, and describes bank-grade security.
- How uptime is measured and any formal service-level definition are not published, so they are not confirmed.

## Support terms

- The website states that customer support and onboarding are available 24/7 from real people.
- Hotela Suite includes priority 24/7 support.
- Response times and support service levels are not published, so they are not confirmed.
- Contact channels are listed in the Support and how-to file.

## Limits this assistant must respect

The bot must not promise discounts, custom features, delivery timelines or integrations that are not stated in the knowledge base or in `plans.json`. Anything not published is answered as not confirmed, with an offer to contact the Hotela team.
