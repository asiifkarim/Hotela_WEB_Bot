# Customers

## The customer list

- Hotela keeps a customer list with name, CNIC, email and contact number.
- The list can be filtered by branch.
- Customer records are the same records that reservations, invoices and events refer to by customer name.

## Guests in the hotel

- Guest Management is part of the Hotela Stay plan.
- Guests and their requests are handled from the front desk, alongside check-in and check-out.
- Guest data belongs to the hotel and is visible only to authorized users in the Hotela dashboard.

## Where customers appear in Hotela

- On room reservations, as the customer name on the reservation.
- On hotel and restaurant invoices, as the name the bill was issued to.
- On events, as the customer behind a wedding or other event.
- On the dashboard, through the booking and financial totals that those records feed.

## What is not documented about customers

- Importing customers from another system.
- Customer history, customer segmentation and marketing lists.
- A customer portal or any customer-facing login.
- Retention periods for customer and booking data inside the Hotela product.
