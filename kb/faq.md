# Visitor FAQ

## What is Hotela?

Hotela is a hotel and restaurant management platform by IdeoMetriX. It brings hotel operations and restaurant operations into one portal: reservations, rooms, restaurant billing, accounts, staff, inventory and customers.

## Which businesses is Hotela for?

Hotels and restaurants, including businesses that run more than one branch and venues that host events such as weddings.

## Does Hotela work for both hotels and restaurants?

Yes. Hotela Stay covers hotel operations such as rooms, reservations and check-in/check-out, and Hotela Dine covers restaurants with table reservations, POS billing, menus, kitchen tickets and online orders. Hotela Suite runs both from one place.

## How long does setup take?

The website says most properties are live within a day: add the property details, invite the managers, and the business is ready to take bookings.

## Can I manage multiple properties from one account?

Yes. Hotela manages multiple properties from one account: users switch between hotels, restaurants and cities from a single dashboard, with role-based access for every team member.

## How much does Hotela cost?

Plan prices are in PKR, per branch, billed every six months, and the current amounts are on the pricing page. Annual prices are not published, and the price for additional branches beyond the first is not published.

## Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial, with full access and no charge to begin.

## Can I cancel anytime?

The website states that customers can cancel anytime. Refund terms and the detailed cancellation steps are not published, so those go to the Hotela team.

## How many branches can I add?

The pricing page asks how many branches the business has and says to contact Hotela after signup when more are needed. The exact branch limit per plan is not published.

## Does Hotela connect to booking websites?

Yes. Hotela includes a Channel Manager that syncs availability and rates across OTAs and booking platforms, and the website states this prevents double bookings. Which individual booking platforms are connected is not published.

## Can Hotela track events like weddings?

Yes. Event Management tracks the event type, date, guest count, revenue and pending balance, with event expenses and a calendar.

## Can Hotela track expenses and profit?

Yes. Hotela shows revenue, expenses, net profit and outstanding balance, records expenses and petty cash, and keeps cash or bank payment records.

## Can Hotela manage staff salaries?

Yes. Staff & Payroll covers employees, current payroll, salary advances, payroll history and final settlement.

## Can Hotela manage inventory?

Yes. Inventory tracks items, categories and quantities, and shows the number of low stock items.

## Does Hotela have housekeeping?

Yes. Housekeeping lists dirty rooms with checkout date and time, tracks cleaning tasks and has an Available Rooms tab.

## What does the Hotela dashboard show?

A financial overview, a booking overview, complaint counts, upcoming check-ins, statistics charts and a transaction report.

## How reliable and secure is Hotela?

The website states 99.9% uptime and that data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with automated backups and granular permissions. How uptime is measured and any service-level definition are not published.

## What support does Hotela offer?

Support and onboarding are available 24/7 from real people, and Hotela Suite includes priority 24/7 support. Response times and service levels are not published.

## Does Hotela have a mobile app?

This is not confirmed. The website footer shows App Store and Google Play buttons, but they are placeholders that do not link to a store listing. For a mobile app, contact the Hotela team.

## Does Hotela work offline?

The website describes offline-ready POS and front desk, plus a Windows and Mac desktop app and real-time cloud sync. Which screens work offline and how syncing behaves are not published.

## Does Hotela support tax invoicing or online payments?

This is not confirmed: tax and e-invoicing configuration, accounting software integration, and payment gateways or online payment collection are not documented. For these, contact the Hotela team.

## How can I get a demo or contact Hotela?

Email the Hotela team at hotelaapp@gmail.com or call +92 355 5871667. Hotela is also on Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn.

## Hotela kya hai?

Hotela hotels aur restaurants ke liye management platform hai, jo IdeoMetriX banata hai. Is mein reservations, rooms, restaurant billing, accounts, staff, inventory aur customers ek hi portal mein hote hain.

## Hotela mein kaun kaun se plans hain?

Hotela ke teen plans hain: Hotela Dine restaurants ke liye, Hotela Stay hotels ke liye, aur Hotela Suite jis mein dono ke features ke saath unified dashboard, multi-branch & roles, channel manager aur priority 24/7 support shamil hain.

## Restaurant ke liye Hotela mein kya features hain?

Hotela Dine mein table reservations, POS & billing, menu aur kitchen tickets, online orders aur restaurant analytics shamil hain.

## Hotel ke liye Hotela mein kya features hain?

Hotela Stay mein room reservations, front desk aur check-in, housekeeping, channel manager aur hotel analytics shamil hain.

## Kya free trial milta hai?

Ji haan. Har plan 7-day free trial ke saath shuru hota hai, jis mein tamam features ka full access milta hai aur shuru karne par koi charge nahi hota.

## Kya main Hotela kisi bhi waqt cancel kar sakta hoon?

Hotela ki website ke mutabiq customers kisi bhi waqt cancel kar sakte hain. Refund aur cancellation ki tafseel publish nahi hai, is liye us ke liye Hotela team se rabta karein.

## Kya multiple branches manage ho sakti hain?

Ji haan. Hotela ek hi account se multiple properties manage karta hai: users ek hi dashboard se hotels, restaurants aur cities ke darmiyan switch karte hain, aur har team member ke liye role-based access hota hai.

## Hotela se rabta kaise karein?

Hotela team ko hotelaapp@gmail.com par email karein ya +92 355 5871667 par call karein. Hotela Facebook, Instagram aur LinkedIn par bhi maujood hai.
