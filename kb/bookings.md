# Bookings

## What counts as a booking in Hotela

Hotela handles three kinds of booking: hotel room reservations, restaurant table reservations, and event bookings such as weddings. Room reservations and table reservations belong to different Hotela plans; events are tracked in Event Management.

## Hotel room reservations

- Hotela keeps one list of all room reservations with reservation number, customer name, room, check-in date, check-out date and status.
- Reservation statuses are Checked In, Checked Out and Cancelled.
- Reservations can be filtered by date range, customer name, branch and status.
- Every reservation has a Manage button for working on that reservation.
- An Available Rooms tab lists the rooms that can be given to a guest.
- Reservations are created and managed from a single calendar, with guests, availability and rates managed from the same place.
- The Hotela home screen shows the number of bookings, available rooms today, total rooms and upcoming check-ins.

## Check-in and check-out

- Check-in and check-out are part of the Hotela Stay plan and the Hotela Suite plan.
- A reservation moves between Checked In and Checked Out as the guest arrives and leaves.
- The front desk shows real-time room status so staff can see which rooms are free.
- Guest requests can be handled from the front desk.

## Restaurant table reservations

- Table reservations are a Hotela Dine feature, so restaurants book tables without the hotel modules.
- The step-by-step table reservation screen is not documented, so its individual fields are not confirmed.

## Event bookings

- Event Management tracks events held at a hotel or restaurant, such as weddings.
- It has three tabs: All Events, Expenses and Calendar.
- Each event shows event ID, event name, customer name, event type, event date, guest count, revenue and pending balance.
- Event types include Wedding.
- The pending balance shows how much of the event amount is still unpaid.
- Events can be filtered by name or ID, branch, event type, status, month, year and date range.
- The Expenses tab tracks the cost of running each event; the money side of events is summarised in the Finances file.
- The Calendar tab shows the events by date.

## Availability and rates across booking websites

- Hotela includes a Channel Manager that syncs availability and rates across OTAs and booking platforms.
- The website states that this keeps availability and rates consistent everywhere and prevents double bookings.
- The channel manager is a feature of the Hotela Stay plan and the Hotela Suite plan.
- The names of the individual OTAs and booking platforms are not published, so those are not confirmed.

## Not confirmed for bookings

- Cancellation, payment and modification rules inside the Manage action. Only the statuses above are documented.
- The fields on the new event form, event packages, menus, venue halls and event booking rules.
- A guest-facing online booking page on the hotel's own website. Hotela records bookings taken by the team; a self-service booking page is not documented.
- Overbooking rules and how the system reacts when two users book the last room.
