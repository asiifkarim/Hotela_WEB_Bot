# Hotela Restaurant Management Features

## What Hotela offers for restaurants

The Hotela Dine plan covers restaurant management: table reservations, POS & billing, menu management, order management, kitchen tickets, online orders and restaurant analytics. The website describes restaurant operations running from the first reservation to the final receipt.

The Hotela dashboard has a Restaurant menu with Food Invoices and Event Management, and the Hotela Suite dashboard shows the same restaurant screens.

## Hotela restaurant billing

- Hotela lists restaurant bills under Food Invoices, with customer name, total items, total discount and total amount.
- Hotela Restaurant Invoices (under Accounts) record items, subtotal, discount, service charge, grand total, received amount and return amount.
- Staff can filter restaurant invoices by date range, customer name and branch.
- The difference between the grand total and the received amount shows what a customer has not yet paid.

## Hotela tables, menus and orders

- Table reservations are part of Hotela Dine.
- Menu management and order management are part of Hotela Dine.
- Kitchen tickets are listed as a Hotela Dine feature on the pricing page.
- Online orders are listed as a Hotela Dine feature on the pricing page.
- The step-by-step screens for menus, orders and table reservations are not documented, so those details are not confirmed.

## Hotela restaurant at multiple branches

Food Invoices can be viewed by branch, so each branch's restaurant sales can be reviewed separately.

## Hotela restaurant and events

A restaurant or hotel can track events such as weddings in Hotela Event Management. See the Hotela events file.

## Hotela plans and restaurant features

- Hotela Dine includes the restaurant features listed above.
- Hotela Suite includes everything in Hotela Dine and everything in Hotela Stay.
