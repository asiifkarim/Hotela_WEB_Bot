# Hotela Multi-Branch Management and Reporting

## Hotela multi-branch management

- Hotela Suite includes Multi-branch Management.
- A business can manage multiple properties from one account: hotels, restaurants and cities all sit behind one login with role-based access.
- Branches are created in Settings under Branches, with a name and a location.
- Most Hotela screens have a Branch filter: reservations, rooms, room categories, transactions, invoices, expenses, food invoices, events, staff, inventory and customers.
- Users can switch between hotels, restaurants and cities from one dashboard, with role-based access for each team member.
- The number of branches depends on the plan. The pricing page lists a branch selector for one to five branches, and when more are needed the owner is asked to contact Hotela support.
- The exact branch limit per plan is not published, so it is not confirmed.

## Hotela reporting and analytics

- The Hotela home screen gives an owner a summary of finances, bookings, complaints and upcoming check-ins.
- A Statistics chart shows reservations or revenue for a chosen year.
- A Transaction Report can be created for a chosen date range.
- The website describes analytics and reports as occupancy, revenue and performance insights.
- Reporting covers every property on the account.

## Hotela complaints overview

The Hotela home screen shows total, resolved and unresolved complaints.

## Not confirmed for reporting

What the Suite dashboard analytics include beyond the screens listed above is not documented. Report export to PDF or Excel is not documented. Neither is confirmed.
