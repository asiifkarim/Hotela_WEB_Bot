## Support

The website states that customer support and onboarding are available 24/7 from real people, and the features page describes 24/7 expert support and onboarding. Hotela Suite includes priority 24/7 support. Response times and service levels are not published.

## Trial and signup

Visitors can start a 7-day free trial at https://portal.hotelaapp.com/signup. The website states that every plan starts with a 7-day free trial, that the trial gives full access to every feature with no charge today, and that customers can cancel anytime.

## What a visitor can do at the signup page

- Create an account and choose a plan.
- Enter the property details to reach the dashboard.
- Invite managers and staff with role-based access.
