# Hotela Feature Availability

## How to use this file

This file lists what is confirmed and what must not be claimed. Confirm a feature only from the groups below. If a visitor asks about a feature in the "not confirmed" group, say the details are not confirmed and offer the Hotela team.

## Confirmed by the Hotela dashboard screens

| Area | Confirmed |
|---|---|
| Reservations | List, filters, statuses (Checked In, Checked Out, Cancelled), Manage action, Available Rooms tab |
| Rooms | Room list, bed types, room categories with cost and occupancy |
| Housekeeping | Dirty rooms list, Available Rooms tab |
| Restaurant | Food Invoices, Restaurant Invoices with discount and service charge |
| Events | Event list, guest count, revenue, pending balance, event expenses, calendar |
| Accounts | Transactions, Hotel and Restaurant Invoices, Expenses, Petty Cash |
| Staff | Employees, Current Payroll, Salary Advances, Payroll History, Final Settlement |
| Inventory | Items, categories, quantity, low stock count |
| Customers | Customer list with name, CNIC, email, contact |
| Branches | Multi-branch management, Branch filter on most screens |
| Dashboard | Financial overview, booking overview, complaints counts, upcoming check-ins, statistics charts, transaction report |
| Subscription | Plans, upgrade, renew, subscription history |

## Confirmed by the pricing page and plans.json

| Feature | Included in |
|---|---|
| Table reservations, POS & billing, Menu & kitchen tickets, Online orders, Restaurant analytics | Hotela Dine |
| Room reservations, Front desk & check-in, Housekeeping, Channel manager, Hotel analytics | Hotela Stay |
| Everything in Stay & Dine, Unified dashboard, Multi-branch & roles, Channel manager, Priority 24/7 support | Hotela Suite |

Plan features are governed by kb/plans.json. Do not claim a plan feature that is not listed there.

## Channel manager

The channel manager is confirmed. The website features page and the pricing page both list it, and it is a Hotela Stay and Hotela Suite feature: it syncs availability and rates across OTAs and booking platforms and the website states that this prevents double bookings. The names of the individual booking platforms are not published.

## Stated on the website, not documented in the product screens

The website describes these, but they are not confirmed by the dashboard screens or the published plan features. Treat them as website statements and do not expand on the details.

- Offline-ready POS and front desk
- A Windows and Mac desktop app
- Works on every device, including access from a phone

## Not confirmed: do not claim these

The following are not confirmed by the website or the product screens. If a visitor asks, say the details are not confirmed and offer the Hotela team.

- A mobile app for iOS or Android. The website footer shows Google Play and App Store buttons, but they are placeholders that do not link to a store listing.
- Annual plan prices. The pricing page offers annual billing but only publishes six-month prices.
- Discounts, refund terms and detailed cancellation steps
- The exact branch limit for each plan
- Tax or government invoicing integration
- Accounting software integration
- Report export to PDF or Excel
- Payment gateway or online payment collection
- SMS, WhatsApp or email notifications to guests
- QR menus or dedicated POS and kitchen display hardware
- API access for developers
- Urdu language interface
- Data import from other systems
- Attendance, leave tracking, payslips, purchase orders, supplier management, customer import and customer segmentation
