# Hotela Hotel Management Features

## What Hotela offers for hotels

Hotela hotel management covers reservations, room setup, housekeeping, guest records and hotel billing. These features are part of the Hotela Stay plan and the Hotela Suite plan.

## Hotela reservations

- Hotela keeps a list of all reservations with reservation number, customer name, room, check-in date, check-out date and status.
- Reservations can be created and managed from a single calendar, with guests, availability and rates managed from the same place.
- Reservation statuses are Checked In, Checked Out and Cancelled.
- Staff can filter reservations by date range, customer name, branch and status.
- Each reservation has a Manage button.
- An Available Rooms tab lists available rooms.
- The Hotela home screen shows the number of bookings, available rooms today, total rooms and upcoming check-ins.

## Hotela check-in and check-out

Hotela Stay includes Check-in/Check-out. Reservations move between the statuses Checked In and Checked Out. The front desk shows real-time room status, and guest requests can be handled from the front desk.

## Hotela rooms and room categories

- Hotela lists every room with room number, bed type and room category.
- Bed types include Single, Double, Twin Double and King size.
- Hotela room categories let a hotel define its own types of rooms, such as Standard, Luxury, VIP or Deluxe.
- Each room category has a cost and an occupancy range.
- Rooms and categories can be viewed by branch.
- Rooms are configured during setup.

## Hotela housekeeping

- Hotela Housekeeping lists dirty rooms with room number, category, bed type, status, checkout date and checkout time.
- Cleaning tasks can be assigned and tracked, with live room status visible to the team.
- Housekeeping also has an Available Rooms tab.

## Hotela channel manager

- Hotela includes a Channel Manager that syncs availability and rates across OTAs and booking platforms.
- The website states that this keeps availability and rates consistent everywhere and prevents double bookings.
- The channel manager is listed as a feature of the Hotela Stay plan and the Hotela Suite plan.
- The names of the individual connected booking platforms are not published, so those are not confirmed.

## Hotela guest management

- Hotela Stay includes Guest Management.
- The Hotela Customers screen lists customers with name, CNIC, email and contact number.
- Customer data belongs to the hotel and is visible only to authorized users in the Hotela dashboard.

## Hotela hotel billing

- Hotela Accounts has a Hotel Invoices tab.
- Reservation payments appear in Hotela Transactions.
- Reporting covers every property on the account.
