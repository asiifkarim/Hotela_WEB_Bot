# Hotela Staff, Inventory and Customer Features

## Hotela Staff & Payroll

- The website describes HR Management as staff schedules, payroll and records for every department in one place.
- Hotela shows Total Employees, Active Staff, On Leave and New Joiners.
- The Employees tab lists staff with name, employee ID, role, branch, joining date and status.
- Staff can be filtered by search, role, status and branch.
- Owners can add a new employee with the Add New Employee button.
- Other tabs are Current Payroll, Salary Advances, Payroll History and Final Settlement.
- Salaries and advances also appear in Hotela Accounts under Expenses.
- Managers and staff are invited with role-based access.

## Hotela Inventory

- Hotela shows Total Items, Low Stock Items and Total Categories.
- Items can be searched by name and filtered by category.
- Each item shows category, quantity, unit and status.
- Owners can add items and increase or decrease quantities.

## Hotela Customers

- Hotela keeps a customer list with name, CNIC, email and contact number.
- The customer list can be filtered by branch.
- Guest data and guest requests from the front desk are part of the customer and front-desk screens.
- Customer data belongs to the hotel and is visible only to authorized users in the Hotela dashboard.

## Not confirmed for staff, inventory and customers

Attendance, leave tracking and payslips are not documented. Low stock alerts, purchase orders and supplier management are not documented. Customer import, customer history and customer segmentation are not documented. None of these are confirmed.
