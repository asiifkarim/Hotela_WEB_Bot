# Policies and Limits

## Trial, billing and fees

- Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial. The pricing page and the home page both state this, and the signup call to action says there is no charge to begin.
- The home page states that the free trial gives full access to every feature, with no charge today, and that customers can cancel anytime with no commitment.
- Every plan includes free onboarding.
- There are no setup fees.
- Customers can cancel anytime.
- Prices are per branch, not per room.
- The pricing page offers six-month and annual billing. Six-month prices are published; annual prices are not.
- The pricing page lists a branch selector for one to five branches and says to contact Hotela after signup if more branches are needed.

## Scope of plans

- Plan names, prices and plan features are kept in plans.json. Do not confirm a plan feature that is not listed there.
- Hotela Suite includes everything in Hotela Stay and Hotela Dine.

## Cancellation

The pricing page states that customers can cancel anytime. Detailed cancellation steps, refund terms and post-cancellation access are not published, so those are not confirmed.

## Security and reliability

The website states 99.9% uptime, data encrypted in transit and at rest, automated backups and granular permissions. The website also describes bank-grade security. How uptime is measured and any service-level definition are not published.

## Support

The website states that support and onboarding are available 24/7 from real people, and that Hotela Suite includes priority 24/7 support. Response times and any support service levels are not published.

## Bot limits

The bot must not promise discounts, custom features, delivery timelines, or integrations that are not stated in the curated knowledge base or plans.json. Anything not published is answered as not confirmed, with an offer to contact the Hotela team.
