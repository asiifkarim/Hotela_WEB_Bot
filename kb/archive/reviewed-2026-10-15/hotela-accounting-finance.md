# Hotela Accounting and Finance Features

## What Hotela offers for accounts

Hotela Accounts has three screens: Transactions, Invoices and Expenses. The Hotela home screen also shows a Financial Overview. The website describes accounts as invoices, expenses, transactions and cash or bank payment records, with reporting across every property.

## Hotela Transactions

- Hotela shows Total Revenue, Total Expenses, Net Profit and Outstanding Balance.
- Transactions include cash and bank payment records.
- Staff can filter by month, year and branch.
- Every transaction shows ID, description, amount and status, with a View button.
- Transaction types include Reservation Payment and Event Payment.

## Hotela Invoices

- Hotela has Hotel Invoices and Restaurant Invoices.
- Restaurant invoices record items, subtotal, discount, service charge, grand total, received amount and return amount.

## Hotela Expenses and petty cash

- Hotela records expenses with name, category, vendor, source and date.
- Hotela has an Expenses tab and a Petty Cash tab.
- Hotela shows Total Petty Cash, Total Expenses and Available Amount.
- Staff salaries and salary advances appear as expenses.

## Hotela dashboard financial overview

The Hotela home screen shows Total Profit, Total Expenses and Available Amount. A Statistics chart shows reservations and revenue by year. A Transaction Report lets the owner choose a start date and end date.

## Not confirmed for accounts

Tax configuration, tax invoicing and government integrations are not documented. Accounting software integrations and report export formats such as PDF or Excel are not documented. Payment methods and payment gateways are not documented. None of these are confirmed.
