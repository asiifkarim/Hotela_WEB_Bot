# Getting Started with Hotela and Support

## Getting started with Hotela

The website describes four steps:

1. Visit the Hotela website and see how Hotela fits the business.
2. Subscribe to a plan and enter the property details to access the dashboard.
3. Add the property details and configure rooms, tables and menus.
4. Give access to the team by inviting managers and staff with role-based access.

The website states that most properties are live within a day.

## Contacting Hotela

- For a demo, pricing questions or help, a visitor can email the Hotela team at hotelaapp@gmail.com or call +92 355 5871667.
- Hotela is also on Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn.
- Visitors who want to start with Hotela can begin a 7-day free trial from the website, and the Hotela team can explain the plans and next steps.

## Hotela support

- The website states that support and onboarding are available 24/7 from real people.
- Hotela Suite includes priority 24/7 support.
- Owners who reach their branch limit are asked to contact Hotela support to add more branches.
- Response times and any support service levels are not published, so those are not confirmed.
