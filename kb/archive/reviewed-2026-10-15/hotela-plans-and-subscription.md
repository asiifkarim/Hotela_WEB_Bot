# Hotela Plans and Subscription

## Where plan facts come from

kb/plans.json is the only source for plan names, prices, billing period and plan features, and it mirrors the public pricing page. A plan fact that is not in plans.json is not confirmed: say it is not confirmed and offer the Hotela team.

## Hotela plans

Hotela has three plans: Hotela Dine, Hotela Stay and Hotela Suite.

| Plan | Positioned for | Features |
|---|---|---|
| Hotela Dine | Restaurants | Table reservations, POS & billing, Menu & kitchen tickets, Online orders, Restaurant analytics |
| Hotela Stay | Hotels | Room reservations, Front desk & check-in, Housekeeping, Channel manager, Hotel analytics |
| Hotela Suite | Hotel + restaurant | Everything in Stay & Dine, Unified dashboard, Multi-branch & roles, Channel manager, Priority 24/7 support |

Hotela Suite is the full platform. It includes the Hotela Stay features and the Hotela Dine features, and adds a unified dashboard, multi-branch management with roles, and priority 24/7 support. A visitor does not need to buy Stay and Dine separately to run a hotel and a restaurant on Suite.

## Hotela plan prices and billing

- Prices and billing periods are kept in plans.json. Always use plans.json and never guess a price.
- Prices are per branch, not per room. The pricing page states this for every plan.
- Plans are billed every six months.
- The pricing page also offers annual billing. Annual prices are not published, so annual pricing is not confirmed; ask the Hotela team.
- The pricing page lists a branch selector for one to five branches and says to contact Hotela after signup if more branches are needed.
- Every plan includes a 7-day free trial, free onboarding and no setup fees, and customers can cancel anytime.

## Choosing a Hotela plan

- A restaurant that needs table reservations, POS billing, menus, kitchen tickets and online orders looks at Hotela Dine.
- A hotel that needs room reservations, front desk and check-in, housekeeping and a channel manager looks at Hotela Stay.
- A business that needs the full platform, or runs several branches, looks at Hotela Suite.

## Managing a Hotela subscription

- In Settings, the Subscription & Billing screen shows the available plans and marks the current plan.
- The current plan has a View / Renew button, and the other plans have Upgrade buttons.
- A subscription history table lists each renewal or reactivation with date, plan, duration, amount, payment method, status and period.

## Hotela cancellation

The Hotela website states that customers can cancel anytime. Refund terms and detailed cancellation steps are not published, so those are not confirmed; ask the Hotela team.
