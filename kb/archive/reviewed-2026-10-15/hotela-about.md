# About Hotela

## What Hotela is

Hotela is a web-based management system for hotels and restaurants. It brings reservations, rooms, restaurant billing, accounts, staff, inventory and customers into one portal. Hotela is a product by IdeoMetriX, a software company based in Pakistan.

## What Hotela helps a business manage

- Room reservations, check-ins and check-outs
- Rooms, room categories and housekeeping
- Restaurant billing, menus, orders and table reservations
- Events such as weddings
- Accounts: transactions, invoices, expenses and petty cash
- Staff, payroll and salary advances
- Inventory and stock
- Customer records
- Multiple branches from one portal

## Who Hotela is for

Hotela is for hotels and restaurants, including businesses that run more than one branch and venues that host events such as weddings.

## Hotela plans

Hotela has three plans: Hotela Dine for restaurants, Hotela Stay for hotels and Hotela Suite for businesses that run both or run several branches. Plan names, prices and plan features are kept in plans.json. Use plans.json for every plan fact and do not describe a plan feature that is not listed there.

## Hotela at a glance

- One portal for hotel and restaurant operations.
- A dashboard home screen with financial overview, booking overview, complaints and upcoming check-ins.
- A Branch filter on most screens for multi-branch businesses.
- Invoices with discount and service charge for restaurant billing.
- Event tracking with guest count, revenue and pending balance.
- Staff payroll with salary advances and final settlement.
