# Hotela Visitor FAQ

## What is Hotela?
Hotela is a web-based management system for hotels and restaurants. It combines reservations, rooms, restaurant billing, accounts, staff, inventory and customers in one portal. Hotela is a product by IdeoMetriX.

## Who is Hotela for?
Hotela is for hotels and restaurants, including businesses with more than one branch and venues that host events such as weddings.

## What features does Hotela offer for hotels?
Hotela offers reservations with statuses and filters, check-in and check-out, rooms and room categories, housekeeping, guest management, hotel invoices and a dashboard with booking overview. Hotel features are part of Hotela Stay and Hotela Suite.

## What features does Hotela offer for restaurants?
Hotela Dine covers restaurant management: table reservations, POS and billing, menu management, order management, kitchen tickets, online orders and restaurant analytics. Hotela also records restaurant invoices with discount, service charge, received amount and return amount.

## Can Hotela manage both a hotel and a restaurant?
Yes. Hotela Stay covers hotel operations and Hotela Dine covers restaurants. Hotela Suite includes everything in Hotela Stay and Hotela Dine, so one Suite subscription runs both from a unified dashboard.

## What plans does Hotela have?
Hotela has three plans: Hotela Dine for restaurants, Hotela Stay for hotels, and Hotela Suite for businesses that run both or run several branches. Hotela Suite includes everything in Hotela Stay and Hotela Dine, plus a unified dashboard, multi-branch management with roles and priority 24/7 support.

## How much does Hotela cost?
Hotela plans are billed every six months and prices are per branch, not per room. The current price of each plan is in the plans list. Annual billing is also offered, but annual prices are not published.

## Are prices per branch or per room?
Per branch, not per room, and the pricing page states this for every plan. The branch limit for each plan is not published.

## Is there a free trial?
Yes. Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial with full access to every feature and no charge to begin. You can cancel anytime.

## Can I cancel Hotela anytime?
The Hotela website states that customers can cancel anytime with no commitment. Refund terms and cancellation steps are not published, so please contact the Hotela team for those.

## How long does it take to get set up?
The website says most properties are live within a day. Getting started has four steps: visit the website, subscribe to a plan and enter your property details, configure rooms, tables and menus, then invite managers and staff with role-based access.

## Does Hotela support multiple branches?
Yes. Hotela Suite includes multi-branch management, and most Hotela screens have a Branch filter. The pricing page offers one to five branches and asks you to contact Hotela for more. The exact branch limit for each plan is not published.

## Does Hotela connect to booking websites or a channel manager?
Yes. Hotela includes a Channel Manager that syncs availability and rates across OTAs and booking platforms, and the website states that this prevents double bookings. It is part of Hotela Stay and Hotela Suite. The names of the individual booking platforms are not published.

## Can I manage multiple properties from one account?
Yes. Hotela manages multiple properties from one account: users switch between hotels, restaurants and cities from a single dashboard, with role-based access for every member of the team.

## Can I use role-based access?
Yes. Managers and staff are invited with role-based access, so each team member sees the parts of Hotela they need.

## Can Hotela track events like weddings?
Yes. Hotela Event Management tracks event type, date, guest count, revenue and pending balance, and has event expenses and a calendar.

## Can Hotela track expenses and profit?
Yes. Hotela shows revenue, expenses, net profit and outstanding balance, and records expenses, petty cash and cash or bank payment records.

## Can Hotela manage staff salaries?
Yes. Hotela Staff & Payroll includes employees, current payroll, salary advances, payroll history and final settlement. Staff schedules and records are part of Hotela HR Management.

## Can Hotela manage inventory?
Yes. Hotela Inventory tracks items, categories and quantities, and shows the number of low stock items.

## What does the Hotela dashboard show?
The Hotela dashboard shows a financial overview, a booking overview, complaint counts, upcoming check-ins, statistics charts and a transaction report.

## Does Hotela have a front desk module?
Yes. Hotela Front Desk handles check-ins, check-outs and guest requests, and shows real-time room status to the team.

## Does Hotela have housekeeping?
Yes. Hotela Housekeeping lists dirty rooms with checkout date and time, has an Available Rooms tab, and lets the team assign and track cleaning tasks.

## Does Hotela have a mobile app?
A mobile app is not confirmed; please contact the Hotela team at hotelaapp@gmail.com. The website says Hotela works on every device, and there is a Windows and Mac desktop app.

## Does Hotela work offline?
The website says the POS and front desk are offline-ready. The detailed offline behaviour is not documented, so specific offline limits are not confirmed.

## Does Hotela support tax invoicing or online payments?
Tax invoicing and online payment collection are not confirmed. Please contact the Hotela team at hotelaapp@gmail.com.

## How can I get a demo or contact Hotela?
Please email the Hotela team at hotelaapp@gmail.com or call +92 355 5871667. Hotela is also on Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn.

## How reliable is Hotela?
The website mentions 99.9% uptime, automated backups and bank-grade security. Service-level details are not published, so please contact the Hotela team for those.

## Is data secure?
Yes. The website states that data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with automated backups and granular permissions. Detailed security documentation is not published.

## What support does Hotela offer?
The website says support and onboarding are available 24/7 from real people, and Hotela Suite includes priority 24/7 support. Response times are not published, so please contact the Hotela team for those.

## Hotela kya hai?

## Hotela mein kaun kaun se plans hain?
Hotela ke teen plans hain: Hotela Dine, Hotela Stay aur Hotela Suite. Hotela Suite mein Hotela Stay aur Hotela Dine ke tamam features ke saath unified dashboard, multi-branch management aur priority 24/7 support shamil hain.

## Kya Hotela mein free trial hai?
Ji haan. Har plan 7-day free trial ke saath shuru hota hai, poore features ke saath, aur shuru mein koi charge nahi hota. Aap kisi bhi waqt cancel kar sakte hain.

## Restaurant ke liye Hotela mein kya features hain?
Hotela Dine mein table reservations, POS aur billing, menu management, order management, kitchen tickets, online orders aur restaurant analytics shamil hain. Hotela restaurant invoices mein discount, service charge, received amount aur return amount bhi record karta hai.

## Hotel ke liye Hotela mein kya features hain?
Hotela mein reservations, check-in aur check-out, rooms aur room categories, housekeeping, guest management aur hotel invoices shamil hain.

## Kya Hotela booking websites se connect hota hai?
Ji haan. Hotela mein Channel Manager hai jo OTAs aur booking platforms ke saath availability aur rates sync karta hai, aur website ke mutabiq is se double booking nahi hoti. Yeh Hotela Stay aur Hotela Suite mein shamil hai.

## Hotela ki price kitni hai?
Hotela ke plans chhe mahine ke liye billed hote hain aur price per branch hai, per room nahi. Har plan ki mojooda price plans list mein hai. Annual billing bhi mojood hai, lekin annual prices publish nahi hain.

## Kya Hotela mein multiple branches manage ho sakti hain?
Ji haan. Hotela Suite mein multi-branch management shamil hai, aur zyada tar screens par Branch filter maujood hai.

## Kya main Hotela kisi bhi waqt cancel kar sakta hoon?
Hotela ki website ke mutabiq customers kisi bhi waqt cancel kar sakte hain. Refund aur cancellation ki tafseel publish nahi hai, is liye Hotela team se rabta karein.
