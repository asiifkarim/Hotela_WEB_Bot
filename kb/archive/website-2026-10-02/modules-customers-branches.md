# Customers and Branches

## Customers and guests

- Manage guest data.
- Manage guest requests from the front desk.
- The website describes the platform as supporting guest and diner experiences.

## Branches and properties

- Manage multiple properties from one account.
- Switch between hotels, restaurants, and cities from one dashboard.
- Use multi-property and role-based access.
- Pricing is per branch, not per room, according to the pricing page.
- The pricing page offers selectors for one through five branches and says to contact Hotela after signup if more are needed.
