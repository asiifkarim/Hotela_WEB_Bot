# About Hotela

Hotela is a hotel and restaurant management platform by IdeoMetriX. It brings hotel operations and restaurant operations into one platform.

## About Hotela

Hotela is designed to simplify operations and improve guest and diner experiences. It supports reservations, guest data, billing, front desk work, restaurant operations, staff management, accounts, and reporting.

## Values

- Reliability: the website states 99.9% uptime, automated backups, and bank-grade security.
- Simplicity: the website says the tools are designed to be mastered in minutes.
- Support: the website states that human support is available 24/7 from onboarding through busy seasons.

## Getting started

The website describes four steps: visit the website, subscribe to a plan, add hotel details, and give access to the team. Users can configure rooms, tables, and menus and invite managers and staff with role-based access.
