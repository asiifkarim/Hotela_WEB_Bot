# FAQ

## Does Hotela work for hotels and restaurants?

Yes. Hotela combines hotel operations, reservations, housekeeping, and front desk with restaurant management, table booking, POS billing, and kitchen tickets.

## How long does setup take?

The website says most properties are live within a day. It describes adding property details, inviting managers, and becoming ready to take bookings.

## Can I manage multiple properties from one account?

Yes. The website says users can switch between hotels, restaurants, and cities from one dashboard with role-based access.

## Does Hotela integrate with booking channels?

The website says Hotela syncs availability and rates across popular OTAs and booking platforms.

## Is data secure?

The website says data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with automated backups and granular permissions.

## Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial. The website says there is no charge to begin and that users can cancel anytime.

## Are prices per branch or per room?

The pricing page says prices are per branch. Hotela Stay is specifically described as priced per branch, not per room.
