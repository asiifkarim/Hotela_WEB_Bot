# Reservations

## Reservations

- Create and manage room reservations from a single calendar.
- Manage guests, availability, and rates.
- Support secure bookings.
- Manage front-desk workflows.
- The channel manager syncs availability and rates across OTAs and booking platforms.
- The website says channel synchronization helps prevent double bookings.

## Online booking

The website offers a 7-day free trial and links visitors to start an account. Specific booking-channel names are not listed.

## Channel Manager

The channel manager syncs availability and rates across popular OTAs and booking platforms. The website says this helps keep availability and rates consistent and prevents double bookings.
