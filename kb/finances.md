# Finances

## The Accounts area

Hotela Accounts has three screens: Transactions, Invoices and Expenses, plus a Petty Cash tab. Invoices themselves are covered in the Invoices file.

## Transactions

- Accounts & Billing covers invoices, expenses and transactions, with cash or bank payment records and reporting across every property.
- Hotela shows Total Revenue, Total Expenses, Net Profit and Outstanding Balance.
- Staff can filter transactions by month, year and branch.
- Every transaction shows ID, description, amount and status, with a View button.
- Transaction types include Reservation Payment and Event Payment.

## Expenses and petty cash

- Hotela records expenses with name, category, vendor, source and date.
- The Expenses tab and the Petty Cash tab show Total Petty Cash, Total Expenses and Available Amount.
- Staff salaries and salary advances appear as expenses, so payroll shows up in the same accounts picture.

## Events and money

- Every event shows its revenue and the pending balance that is still unpaid.
- Event payments appear in Transactions as Event Payment.
- The cost of running an event is tracked on the event's own Expenses tab.

## Dashboard financial overview

- The Hotela home screen shows Total Profit, Total Expenses and Available Amount.
- A Statistics chart shows reservations and revenue for a chosen year.
- A Transaction Report can be produced for a chosen start date and end date.
- Reporting covers every property on the account, so a multi-branch business can review the branches together.

## What is not documented about finances

- Tax configuration, tax invoicing and government integrations.
- Accounting software integration and report export formats such as PDF or Excel.
- Payment methods and payment gateways: how a guest pays, and whether card or online payment is collected in Hotela.
- Retention periods for financial records.
- Purchase orders, supplier management and vendor payments beyond the expense vendor field.
